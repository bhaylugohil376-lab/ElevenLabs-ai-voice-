Pricing FAQ

Is the Free plan really limited to 5 AI uses?

Yes. The application is designed to enforce 5 AI processing uses per day for Free users on the server side.

Is Premium truly unlimited?

Premium can have unlimited application-level access, but third-party AI providers can still impose API, credit, rate or infrastructure limits.

Can I cancel Premium?

Cancellation depends on the payment provider used by the production deployment. The subscription status should be updated through the payment webhook.

Does upgrading immediately change my account?

After successful payment, the payment webhook should update your subscription record and your account will receive the Premium plan.

VoiceAI pricing shown here is the application pricing. AI provider usage and infrastructure costs are separate.
