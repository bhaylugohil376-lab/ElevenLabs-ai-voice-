Pricing FAQ

Can I use VoiceAI for free?

Yes. The Free plan gives access to the major tools with a limit of 5 AI processing actions per 24 hours.

How much is Premium?

Premium is $20 per month on the monthly plan. Final billing and taxes depend on your payment provider.

Are all AI tools included?

The interface can expose all major tools. Actual AI processing depends on the backend and provider connected to the VoiceAI application.

Can I cancel Premium?

Yes, Premium is designed as a monthly subscription and can be cancelled through the connected billing system.

Does Premium mean literally unlimited?

Premium removes the normal Free-plan daily limit, but real provider, server, storage and fair-use limits may still apply.
