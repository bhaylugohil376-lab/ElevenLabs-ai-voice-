What does the free plan include?

Free users can access the available sections with a limit of 5 AI uses per day. The daily allowance resets.

Can I use all sections for free?

Yes. The website can expose all sections to free users. AI processing limits are applied separately.

What does Premium unlock?

Premium is designed for higher usage, premium voices and advanced tools.

Can the AI API be changed later?

Yes. The final SaaS can be connected to the seller's own server and API provider.
