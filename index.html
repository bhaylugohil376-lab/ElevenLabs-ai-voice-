FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use VoiceAI for free?

Yes. The planned Free plan includes limited AI usage, currently shown as 5 AI uses per 24 hours.

Are the voices real human voices?

The voice library can contain natural AI voices. Actual generated audio depends on the connected AI voice provider and backend.

Can I download generated audio?

Yes. Tool pages with generated audio will include a Download option when the backend returns an audio file.

Is VoiceAI available on mobile?

The interface is designed to be responsive for both desktop and mobile devices.

Does Google login work automatically?

Google login requires a connected authentication backend and Google OAuth configuration.
