ABOUT VOICEFORGE AI

Simple voice creation for everyone

VoiceForge AI is an independent AI voice platform designed to make text-to-speech simple and accessible for creators, students, educators, businesses and video makers.

Users can write or paste text, select a language and voice, adjust speech speed and preview the generated speech.

The platform is designed with a multilingual architecture so additional languages and professional AI voice services can be added in future versions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is VoiceForge AI free?

The browser voice preview can work without a paid API. Advanced AI voice generation may require a supported third-party TTS service and its applicable usage limits.

Which languages are supported?

The interface includes Hindi, English, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Arabic.

Can I use it for YouTube videos?

You can use generated audio according to the terms and commercial-use permissions of the voice provider you use.

Will more features be added?

Yes. The project structure is designed so features such as accounts, credits, dubbing, speech-to-text and additional voice services can be added later.
