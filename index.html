FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use VoiceAI for free?

Yes. The Free plan can provide limited AI usage, currently planned at 5 AI uses per 24 hours.

Are the voices real human voices?

VoiceAI uses natural AI-generated voices from the connected voice provider. The available library depends on your configured provider account.

Can I download generated audio?

Yes. Successful audio generation provides an audio player and a Download Audio option.

Is VoiceAI available on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Does Google login work automatically?

Google login requires the Google OAuth backend configuration to be completed.
