FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about the VoiceAI platform.

Is VoiceAI free? +

Yes. VoiceAI is planned with a free plan that provides access to supported tools with a daily usage limit.

How many free AI uses are available? +

The planned free limit is 5 AI uses per 24-hour period. The exact limit can be controlled by the backend.

How much is Premium? +

The planned Premium price is $20 per month with additional features and higher usage limits.

Can I use VoiceAI on mobile? +

Yes. The interface is designed to work across mobile, tablet and PC.

Can I download generated files? +

Yes. Supported AI tools will include download options for generated outputs.

Is Text to Image available? +

Yes. Text to Image has its own dedicated workspace at image.html.
